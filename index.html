About Me

I am a striving young university student of Nueva Vizcaya State University (NVSU). I love music, hanging out with my friends, and enjoy Life as I live it a day at a time. I chose to study for the college course "Bachelor of Science in Information Technology - Web and Mobile Applications Development" (BSIT-WMAD) to learn about coding, network with my fellow learners that wants to further their career, and try my best to make my family and friends proud.

History in NVSU

1st Year - 1st Semester

Very exciting 1st step as a college student. I was part of the section BSIT-1A and together with the class we were able to tackle our first introduction to our course, specifically the two major courses, Introduction to Computing, and Computer Programming 1. Other than the major subjects, these are the other courses that I took:

  • Environmental Science
  • Mathematics in the Modern World
  • Understanding the Self
  • Art Aprreciation
  • Physical Activities Towards Health and Fitness 1
  • ROTC - Basic Course

1st Year - 2nd Semester

A coninuation of my 1st school year as part of the BSIT-1A section. After passing all my courses in the 1st semester, the next majors I had to take were, Computer Programming 2, Discrete Mathematics, and Data Structures and Algorithms. Here are the other courses:

  • Philippine Indigenous Community
  • Science, Technology, and Society
  • The Contemporary World
  • Physical Activities Towards Health and Fitness 2
  • ROTC 2 - Basic Course

2nd Year - 1st Semester

My latest step forward for my college journey and my new section is that of BSIT - 2D. I now have both new and old classmates to work together with as we come forward with four major courses - "Webpage Design Using HTML 5", "Information Management", "Platform Technologies" and "Object Oriented Programming. Collaborating with the challenges of new topics to tackle, are these minor subjects:

  • Ethics
  • Readings in Philippine History
  • The Entrepreneurial Mind
  • Physical Activities Towards Health and Fitness 3
